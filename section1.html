How can I trust God even when life gets hard?

What do I do when I feel like giving up on my Faith?

Being a young Christian definitely isn't easy, sometimes life gets hard and it feels like God isn't there, He's silent. But that's never the case! God is always near you! In Deuteronomy 31:8 it states "The Lord himself goes before you and will be with you; he will never leave you nor forsake you. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged" so continue to keep seeking God and talking to Him when you feel lost, He will never abandon you.

You don't have to have everything figured out

Trusting God doesn't mean that you will never have questions or doubts. It means choosing to keep going and remembering that you don't have to face everything alone. Take your worries to God through prayer and allow Him to guide you one step at a time.

"When life gets hard, remember that you don't have to walk through it alone."
