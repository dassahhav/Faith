Why does following God feel difficult sometimes?

God is still with you

Life can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially when things don't go the way we planned. But difficult seasons don't mean that God has left you. Even when you cannot see what He is doing, you can continue to trust that He is with you.

You don't have to have everything figured out

Trusting God doesn't mean that you will never have questions or doubts. It means choosing to keep going and remembering that you don't have to face everything alone. Take your worries to God through prayer and allow Him to guide you one step at a time.

"When life gets hard, remember that you don't have to walk through it alone."
