Is it normal to doubt my faith in God?

Having doubts with your faith is normal!

Being a young Christian will make you have doubts about God, especially if you see others who don't believe in God being more blessed than you are. You may begin to question why God seems to answer their prayers while yours remain unanswered, or why they seem to have an easier and more successful life despite not following Him. However, faith is not about comparing your life to someone else's. God’s plan for each person is different, and what we see as a blessing may not always be what is truly best for us. Instead of allowing comparison to weaken your faith, use those moments of doubt to seek God more deeply, ask questions, and grow in your relationship with Him. Having doubts does not make you a bad Christian; sometimes, they can lead you to a stronger and more genuine faith.

What to do when you are doubting

  • Take a break from everything, give your brain some quiet time
  • Pray anyways. Even if you don't feel like praying, just take a couple of minutes out of your day to ask God for guidance
  • Read scriptures. You don't have to read so many chapters, but it could be a few verses.
  • Write down what made you doubt God. Was it something you saw? Something that happened? Fear?
  • Listen to worship music that puts you at peace
  • Talk to someone who will understand what you mean.

    • James 1:6-8 "But when you pray, you must believe and not doubt at all. Whoever doubts is like a wave in the sea that is driven and blown about by the wind.
